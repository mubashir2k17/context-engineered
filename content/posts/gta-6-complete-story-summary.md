# Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA 6): Complete Story Summary

### Chapter 1: Bad Luck & New Beginnings
![Lucia Caminos in Hamlet Correctional Facility](/images/gta6/chapter-1.jpg)

The story opens with how **Jason Duval** and **Lucia Caminos** first met: Jason steals a car for his mother, and Lucia jumps in to help him bluff their way past a police stop. The two quickly become a couple, though Lucia grows frustrated when Jason refuses to include her in his criminal jobs. After a heated argument, Lucia walks out—unbeknownst to Jason, she is pregnant with their son, **Tommy**.

Struggling to survive on her own, Lucia impulsively robs a coffee shop. She flees in a panic and accidentally leaves her infant son behind, turning back to get him and getting arrested. After nine months in Hamlet Correctional Facility, prison overcrowding earns Lucia an early supervised release with an ankle monitor. She moves into Jason’s rundown house in the Keys, where Jason works as a drug smuggler alongside his conspiracy-obsessed friend **Cal** for a local boss named **Brian**. Things take a dark turn when a smuggling run goes south against a ruthless river gang called the **Kingfishers**. During a botched peace negotiation, Brian’s pilot **Donnie** is killed by the gang, prompting Brian to retire from the drug trade and leaving Jason and Lucia desperate for cash.

### Chapter 2: The Visitor Center Ambush
![The Grass Rivers Visitor Center Ambush](/images/gta6/chapter-2.jpg)

Desperate to afford a lawyer to regain custody of Tommy, Jason and Lucia take a courier job in Vice City for **Matia**, the boyfriend of Lucia’s prison friend Cat. Wearing disguises, they retrieve a mysterious duffel bag from a locker at the Grass Rivers Visitor Center.

Before they can leave, they are detained by local police—only for a corrupt police task force led by **Captain Aldo Mendes** to storm the building and execute the arresting officers to steal the bag. Jason is critically shot in the crossfire, though Lucia drags him out and escapes with the bag. Hitmen burn down Jason’s house in the Keys, forcing Lucia to break Jason out of a Vice City hospital in a stolen ambulance. Hiding out in a cheap motel, they open the bag to find an antique bronze statue and a **military-grade encrypted hard drive**. While Jason recovers, Lucia teams up with local hustlers—including a club dancer named Vicky and a chaotic veteran named Dunk—to secure painkillers and pull off small-time stickups and car boosts to survive.

### Chapter 3: Vice City Hustle & Raul’s Crew
![Vice City National Bank Heist](/images/gta6/chapter-3.jpg)

Moving into Vice City, Jason reconnects with his old friend **Dre’quan**, who is trying to launch a record label (*Only Raw Records*) out of a strip club owned by a volatile street boss named **Booby**. Jason and Lucia help Dre’quan manage his wild rap duo, **The Dimes** (Roxy and Lux), navigating industry beefs and rival rappers like Downplay.

Meanwhile, through a connection from Brian, Jason and Lucia are recruited by **Raul**, a seasoned career bank robber. Alongside Raul’s tech expert **Kareem**, con-artist **Andrea**, and a coerced vault designer named **Nicole**, they execute a massive daylight heist at the **Vice City National Bank**. Despite a chaotic getaway, the crew pulls off the score, putting real money in Jason and Lucia's pockets for the first time.

### Chapter 4: High Society & Mega Mundo
![Rescuing Rafa at the Cement Factory](/images/gta6/chapter-4.jpg)

Using their bank heist cut, Jason and Lucia check into a five-star Ocean Beach hotel under fake identities ("Mr. and Mrs. Walker") arranged by Raul's money launderer, **Valentina**. They also hire a flashy, hyper-connected lawyer named **Abraham "Abe" Gonzalez** to clear their records and secure custody of Tommy.

To pay Abe's steep fees, Jason and Lucia work undercover as bodyguards for **Andres De Leon**, a narcissistic action star married into the family running **Mega Mundo**, a massive Spanish-language media empire. During Mega Mundo's headquarters launch party, domestic revolutionaries attack the building and later kidnap Andres’s young son, Rafa. Jason and Lucia track the kidnappers to a cement factory, rescue the boy, and expose Mega Mundo’s head of security, Gustavo, as the inside traitor.

### Chapter 5: Infiltrating the Airport & Betrayal
![Vice City International Airport Tarmac Shootout](/images/gta6/chapter-5.jpg)

While Kareem works to decrypt the mystery hard drive, Raul tasks Jason and Lucia with infiltrating a violent crew of gym-obsessed heist criminals led by **Cordell**. Lucia gains their trust by lifting at their exclusive club, while Jason takes a cover job as a baggage handler at **Vice City International Airport** to plant a network cloner.

Raul's crew hijacks Cordell's planned airport robbery of millions in untraceable foreign currency. The job spirals into a gunfight on the tarmac, leaving Cordell dead and Kareem killed in the crossfire. Before dying, Kareem decrypts enough of the hard drive to reveal compromising blackmail footage of **U.S. Senator Camila Fuentes**. Just as they prepare to auction the drive off and disappear, Valentina betrays them to Captain Mendes. Raul is mortally wounded on his boat, their bank accounts are frozen, and Jason and Lucia are left with nothing but the hard drive and the antique statue.

### Chapter 6: Giorgio Gallo & The Double-Cross
![Giorgio Gallo and Niko Bellic](/images/gta6/chapter-6.jpg)

Out of options, Lucia contacts Matia, leading to their capture by **Giorgio Gallo**, a wealthy Italian shipping magnate with ties to the Albanian mob who originally owned the stolen statue and hard drive. Gallo’s fleet was seized by the feds, and he needs the blackmail on Senator Fuentes to get his ships back—and to stop the Albanians (including **Niko Bellic**, who makes a cameo threatening Giorgio) from killing him.

Giorgio forces Jason and Lucia to team up with his eccentric ex-Navy tactical trainers for a massive two-pronged assault: stealing 20 tons of seized cocaine back from a Coast Guard base while simultaneously sabotaging a rival cartel shipment at sea using a drugged celebrity K-9. Having delivered the cocaine and the hard drive, Giorgio promises Jason and Lucia full DOJ pardons arranged through Senator Fuentes. However, as soon as they sign their full confessions with Abe, Giorgio double-crosses them—handing them over to Captain Mendes and the corrupt police as fall guys to clean up his own mess.

### Chapter 7: The Final Stand
![Jason and Lucia's Final Stand](/images/gta6/chapter-7.jpg)

Locked in separate custody, Lucia is offered immunity and witness protection by the statewide prosecutor if she turns on Jason, which she refuses. Meanwhile, Captain Mendes sends cartel hitmen to besiege the Hamlet police station to silence Jason. Lucia escapes custody, fights her way through the precinct siege to rescue Jason, and together they kill Captain Mendes in a final shootout.

Refusing to surrender, Jason and Lucia recruit Brian and Cal for one last assault. They storm Giorgio Gallo’s heavily guarded penthouse, killing Giorgio and his mercenaries and recovering the hard drive. As they race toward a getaway boat arranged by Cal, the police intercept them and block off every escape route. Surrounded with nowhere left to run, Jason urges Lucia to take the hard drive and accept the prosecutor's immunity deal so she can survive and raise their son. Lucia refuses to leave him—declaring they are in this together to the end—as the two floor the gas pedal straight into a hail of police gunfire.
